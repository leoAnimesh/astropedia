### Description (4000)
Astropedia is Vedic astrology that stays on your phone. Draw an accurate kundli, follow your dasha periods, check the day's panchang and ask an on-device astrology guide about your chart, in English, Hindi or Bengali.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics.
• Birth details, charts, chats and your journal are stored only on your phone.
• The astrology guide runs on the phone itself. Your questions are never sent to a server.
• Works offline once the model has downloaded.

YOUR BIRTH CHART
• Lagna (rising), Moon and Sun signs, planets in signs, houses and nakshatras.
• Lahiri ayanamsa with whole-sign houses, calculated by the app's own astronomy.
• Readings draw on planetary strength, yogas and divisional charts.

TIMING
• Vimshottari dasha timeline: where you are now and what comes next.
• Sade sati: whether Saturn's 7.5-year transit is on you, and which phase.
• Transit alerts and a forecast for each person you add.
• Muhurat: good windows for the things you plan.

EVERY DAY
• Panchang: tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, sunrise, Rahu Kaal and choghadiya for your city.
• Daily reading for your sign, and a Bhagavad Gita verse.
• Festival calendar with reminders.

RELATIONSHIPS
• Kundli matching with Ashtakoota (36 gunas), Manglik check and a plain-language explanation.
• Add family and friends and see their charts side by side.

ASK SAGA
• Saga is Astropedia's own small AI model, trained for Vedic astrology. It answers questions about career, love, health, study and timing from your actual chart.
• Dates and calculations come from the app, so the guide can't invent planetary positions.
• Krishna mode offers gentle reflections with a Bhagavad Gita verse.

REPORTS AND JOURNAL
• Life, career, love, health, study and family reports you can save as PDF.
• A private journal that notices patterns with your transits.
• Export a backup file whenever you like; you decide where it goes.

THREE LANGUAGES
English, हिन्दी and বাংলা throughout, including the guide.

Please note: Astropedia is for reflection and curiosity. It is not medical, psychological, legal or financial advice. If you are struggling, call Tele-MANAS on 14416 (free, 24×7) or 112 in an emergency.

The first launch downloads the on-device model (about 200 MB) over the internet. Saga is fine-tuned from Google's Gemma; Gemma is provided under and subject to the Gemma Terms of Use.
