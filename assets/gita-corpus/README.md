## How Telang's text was prepared

Telang wrote continuous prose without verse numbers. It was split into the
700 verses by chapter and verse, using the Sanskrit as the guide. His wording
is kept, with these changes:

- SBE transliteration of names modernised (Arguna → Arjuna, Sañgaya → Sanjaya,
  Ganârdana → Janardana); diacritics dropped; footnotes and page breaks removed.
- Obvious OCR and typesetting slips fixed ("lies at case" → "lies at ease").
- Where Telang folds two or three verses into one sentence in a different
  order from the Sanskrit (2.42–44, 8.9–10, 9.11–12, 12.3–4), each of those
  verses carries the whole sentence.
- Speaker lines are written as "Arjuna said:" and so on. Telang calls Krishna
  "The Deity".
- In five curated verses split mid-sentence, `[brackets]` mark words we
  supplied and "…" marks a sentence that continues in the next verse
  (6.22, 12.13, 12.14, 12.18, 12.19).
- 13.1 (Arjuna's question) is not in the recension Telang translated; its
  English in `verses.json` is Astropedia's own.

Word-by-word meanings are no longer included: nothing in the app showed them.
